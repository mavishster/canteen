# Selling hours by category

Admin, Categories, "Selling hours": for each category an optional time window and optional weekdays, in school time.
Both times are included (12:00 to 13:00 allows a sale at exactly 12:00 and at exactly 13:00). All seven days is the same as no day limit.
Checked on the server inside the sale, after the school's general buying hours: one closed item refuses the whole sale, nothing is charged,
and the message names the category and its hours. Products with no category, and categories with nothing set, are never limited.
